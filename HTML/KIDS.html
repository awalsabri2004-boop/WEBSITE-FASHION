Care Instructions

Easy to care for

Simple care keeps kids' clothes fresh, soft, and ready for the next adventure.

  • Wash: Machine wash cold with similar colors.
  • Dry: Tumble dry on low or hang dry.
  • Iron: Warm iron if needed; avoid prints.
  • Store: Fold neatly to keep ready for wear.
